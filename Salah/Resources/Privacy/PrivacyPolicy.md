# Salah Privacy Policy

Salah is a privacy-first, charitable prayer-time application. It does not require an account and includes no advertising or third-party tracking SDK.

Prayer times and Hijri dates are calculated on your device. Salah does not transmit your coordinate or calculation preferences to its own server or a third-party prayer-time service. Salah requests location only while the app is in use and does not perform continuous or background location tracking. You can decline location access and select one of the bundled Bangladesh districts instead.

Prayer completion records are stored locally using SwiftData. Preferences, reminder choices, and onboarding state are stored locally on the device. Notifications are scheduled through Apple’s local notification system. This release does not synchronize tracker data to iCloud.

Salah does not sell personal data and does not use advertising identifiers. You can permanently clear local prayer-tracker records from More > Privacy & Data.

Prayer-time cache entries are retained on-device for quick access and are replaced automatically when relevant settings change.
